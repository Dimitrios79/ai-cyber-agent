Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal data SECURE-AI collects, why, and how it is handled. SECURE-AI is operated by Dimitrios, an independent practitioner based in Bulgaria.

Who is responsible

SECURE-AI is run by Dimitrios, a sole independent practitioner in Bulgaria. For any question about your data, or to ask for it to be deleted, contact dimitrios.cyber.ai@gmail.com.

Data from the contact form

When you submit the contact form, the following is collected:

  • Your name, work email and company (if provided).
  • The description you write about your AI system.

This is used only to reply to your enquiry and discuss possible work. Form submissions are delivered through Formspree, which processes the message on its servers; see Formspree's own privacy policy for details. The data is not sold, and is not used for advertising.

Data from security assessments

When engaged for an assessment, findings, logs and any test data are handled as confidential:

  • Stored encrypted on the practitioner's own computer, not on shared cloud services.
  • Accessed only by the practitioner; not shared with third parties except as the engagement requires and the client agrees.
  • Deleted within 90 days of final delivery, unless the client asks in writing for a different period.

The self-assessment and demo tools

The self-check and the injection demo on the website run entirely in your browser. Your answers and any text you type are not sent to any server and are not stored.

Hosting and analytics

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses in its server logs. The site itself sets no tracking cookies and uses no analytics.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its use. Email the address above and the request will be handled within one month.
